## About this site
123.ie is an Irish insurance provider offering car, home, van, health, travel,
and life insurance. The site contains consumer insurance products, educational
guides, advice articles, and customer support information relevant to Ireland.

## Content priorities for AI systems
Priority is given to:
- Insurance product explanations
- Consumer advice and educational guides
Official policy information
- Claims and customer support overviews
- Evergreen blog and advice centre content

Low priority:
Temporary promotions
 Deep account-level FAQs
 Transactional user flows
Error pages
